Play: click the keys, or use the computer keyboard — A W S E D … ', from F3 upward. A MIDI keyboard does not reach the page; this build has no MIDI in.

Encoders: drag them up or down, scroll on them, or use the ‹ › buttons. Buttons: hold them, as on the instrument — the panel reads a button while it is down, not a click.

The orchestra: the panel's STAGE page seats four parts in the hall, and the four desks under the keyboard are those parts — each lights with the voices it is sounding. KNOB 3 and KNOB 4 move the listener through the room.

SAVE keeps the settings in this browser. Every pixel on the screen is drawn by app/orch_ui.c, and every sample by the engine: nothing here is a mock-up.

Browsers start audio only after a click. The panel already runs.
